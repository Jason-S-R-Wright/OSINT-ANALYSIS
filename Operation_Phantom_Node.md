### I. Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)
We assess it is **almost certain (95–100%)** that recent digital intelligence depicting armored unit movements along the contested border region is the result of a sophisticated adversarial data-poisoning campaign rather than real-world military positioning. We evaluate it as **highly likely (80–95%)** that a recently resigned cloud infrastructure engineer acted as an insider threat to facilitate this system compromise under duress. If immediate defensive cyber counter-measures are not implemented, national wireless intelligence communication channels face a very high risk of broader operational disruption.

### II. Evaluative Judgments & Analysis of Alternatives (AoA)
*   **Primary Hypothesis (Data Poisoning via Insider Assistance):** The anomalous 4.2-second satellite communication lag paired with the sudden flight of key technical staff indicates intentional network manipulation. The engineer's sudden departure under the guise of familial issues strongly signals compromise-by-proxy (coercion via threats to family).
*   **Alternative Hypothesis (Adversarial Camouflage):** There is an **unlikely/improbable (20–45%)** chance that the adversary is utilizing advanced camouflage and sensor-negation tactics that bypass our physical ground assets while intentionally leaking altered telemetry. We reject this based on trusted human asset reports at the perimeter.
*   **Technical Glitch Alternative:** There is an **unlikely/highly improbable (10–15%)** chance this represents a systemic infrastructure malfunction. The deliberate bypassing of customs and specific infrastructure lease trails strongly undermines a pure technical error theory.

### III. Structural Foundations: Facts vs. Assumptions

#### Verified Facts:
*   Physical border assets and trusted human monitoring teams report zero visible armored movements.
*   The data anomaly includes a persistent 4.2-second telemetry gap tracked to a leased commercial downlink facility in Eastern Europe.
*   The firm's cloud infrastructure engineer resigned abruptly and immediately fled to a non-extradition jurisdiction.

#### Working Assumptions:
*   We assume the engineer's sudden invocation of "family issues" indicates an active threat vector targeting his dependents by a bad actor to secure system access credentials.
*   We assume the engineer’s rapid transit to a non-extradition country was executed specifically to evade immediate federal detection and physical capture.

### IV. Immediate Intelligence Gaps & Targeted Collection Requirements
1.  **CI / law Enforcement:** Coordinate with domestic law enforcement to locate the engineer's immediate family and execute a rapid threat-and-safety assessment.
2.  **SIGINT / Cyber:** Deploy advanced overhead imagery sensors directly over the contested coordinates to establish absolute physical ground truth.
3.  **System Hardening:** Revoke all legacy access keys associated with the departed engineer and audit the wireless broadcast pipeline to isolate and patch the Eastern European relay point.
