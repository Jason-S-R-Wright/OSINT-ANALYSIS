---

## 🛠️ Core Methodologies & Tradecraft Standards
Every intelligence product within this portfolio is built to comply with the mandatory **Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) Intelligence Community Directive 203 (ICD 203)** guidelines. 

Key frameworks utilized throughout these case studies include:
*   **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front):** Structuring briefs so that senior leaders receive definitive conclusions and strategic impacts in the first three sentences.
*   **Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH):** Systematically identifying alternative scenarios to prevent organizational tunnel vision and confirm evidentiary weight.
*   **Structured Analytic Techniques (SATs):** Actively applying Premortem Analyses, Red Hat testing, and Source Evaluation Metrics to stress-test logic before final reporting.
